Would You Rather?

Two answers, one choice. Pick one, see how everyone else chose, and collect points. In Serbian, the game is Шта би радије?

Fly whenever you like

or

Read other people's minds

How it works

  • Play at once. No sign-up: the moment you open the game, you play as a guest.
  • Pick and see the split. Tap an answer and see straight away how the votes split.
  • Collect points. Every answer earns a point, whatever you pick: there is no right or wrong answer.
  • Write your own questions. With an account you can send in a question. Once a moderator approves it, it reaches other players, and every like earns you a point.
  • Pick your topics. Food, travel, superpowers, the absurd and many more categories.
  • Cyrillic or Latin. The game is in Serbian, and you switch the script whenever you like, on the account screen.

Where to play

On Android, from Google Play, in Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro and North Macedonia: [ЛИНК / LINK]. Versions for iPhone and the browser come later.

The game is for people aged 16 and over.

No ads

The game has no ads, and we do not sell your data. What we process, and why, is in the privacy policy.
