2. What data we process and why

Playing as a guest

When you first open the game, the server creates a player and gives it a random id. With it your device gets two session tokens: an access token, valid for 15 minutes, and a refresh token, valid for 30 days and replaced every time it is used. The tokens are kept in the app's storage on the device (in a browser, in its localStorage), and the server keeps only a hash of the refresh token. Each device has its own session. The language you pick is kept on the device only.

Why: so the game knows it is you, without signing up, and keeps your points.

An account (optional)

If you register, we keep with the same player the username you choose (3 to 20 characters) and your password, but only as a hash (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, with a random salt of its own for each password). We cannot read your password, it is never written to any log, and we never send it to anyone. We do not ask for an email address, so we cannot recover a forgotten password.

Why: so you can log in on another device with the same points, and send in your own questions.

Signing in with Google Play Games

If you sign in with Google Play Games in the Android version, Google sends us your Play Games player id, which we link to your account in the game.

Why: so you can sign in without typing a username and password.

Gameplay data

  • your answers: which side of a question you pick (for each question we keep your latest answer) and how long you took to answer;
  • the questions you skip and your reactions to questions (thumbs up or down);
  • your points, how many questions you have answered, and which round of questions you are in;
  • the questions you send in: both answers, categories, status and, if a question is rejected, the reason;
  • the reports of questions you send.

Why: for the game to work. The server picks the questions not yet shown to you in this round, counts your points and shows how players answered. Other players see only totals, never who chose what, and questions are shown without their author's name. We use reports to remove questions that break the rules.

IP address and server logs

Every request from the game reaches our server on Render through Cloudflare's network, so both see your device's IP address and technical details of the request (time, the address requested, the app or browser version). We use the IP address to limit how many requests one address can send, against abuse and password guessing. Those counters are kept only in the server's memory, for an hour at most, and disappear every time the server restarts. Server logs record technical events and errors and, when we refuse a request for going over a limit, the player id. We never log tokens or passwords.

Why: security, and keeping the game running.

Analytics (PostHog, EU)

To see what works in the game and what confuses people, the app sends events about how it is used, for example: a screen opened, a question answered or skipped, how long an answer took, an error. With them go the app version, the device type, the operating system and the game's language. Events are tied to a random analytics id made on the device, not to your username, and hold no name, password or IP address. PostHog processes them, on servers in the European Union.

You turn analytics off in the app, on the Account screen. While it is off, the app sends no events at all.

Why: to fix bugs and make the game better.

Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

On a phone, the game can let you know when a moderator approves your question. If you allow notifications, your phone gets a notification token from Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (on an iPhone, through Apple's notification service), and the app sends it to our server. We use that token only for notifications about your questions. You turn notifications off in your phone's settings, and we delete the token when you log out or delete your account.

Why: so you learn that your question is in the game.

Personalization (planned)

Later we want to pick questions for you by what you enjoy, based on your answers and reactions. That is profiling only to choose which question to show you: never for ads, never sold, and nobody else sees it. It makes no decision with legal effects on you or a similarly significant effect. Questions about religion, politics, health, and sex life or sexual orientation are never used for personalization, and we do not try to infer anything about those topics from your answers. You will be able to turn personalization off in the app.

When you write to us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you and deal with your request.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics, and loads nothing from other services. The hosting provider (Render) may record visitors' IP addresses in its technical logs.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your full name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The game has no ads and no advertising ids.

3. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (Article 12(1)(2) of Serbia's Law on Personal Data Protection; Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): playing as a guest and with an account, sessions, gameplay data, the questions you send in, signing in with Google Play Games, and the notifications you turn on. Without them the game cannot work the way you use it.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 12(1)(6); Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): security and request limits, server logs, moderation and reports, analytics to improve the game and, once it is introduced, personalization. You can object to this processing, and you can turn analytics and personalization off yourself, in the app.
  • Legal obligation (Article 12(1)(3); Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): when the law requires us to keep data or hand it to an authority.

  • Your questions: approved ones stay in the game after your account is deleted, without an author; the rest are deleted with the account.

7. Security

The connection to the server uses HTTPS. We keep passwords only as salted hashes and refresh tokens only as SHA-256 hashes, so even someone who got hold of the database could not read passwords or use tokens from it. On the device, tokens are kept in the app's ordinary storage, not in a separate vault of the operating system, since the game keeps nothing sensitive: anyone with your unlocked device can play as you. Password guesses are limited per IP address. No measure is perfect, so if you notice anything suspicious, write to us.

  • of access: to learn what data about you we process and get a copy;
  • to rectification of inaccurate data;
  • to erasure: you can also delete your account right in the app (how);

  • to data portability: to get the data about your play in a machine-readable form;
  • to object to processing based on legitimate interests, analytics and personalization included;
  • to withdraw consent, where any processing rests on it;
  • to lodge a complaint with Serbia's Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (www.poverenik.rs) and, if you live in the EU, with your own country's supervisory authority.
