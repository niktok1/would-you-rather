1. About the game

Would You Rather? is a game in which you pick one of two options, see how other players chose, and collect points. It is run by [ИМЕ И ПРЕЗИМЕ / NAME], [АДРЕСА / ADDRESS] ("we").

The game is in Serbian, in Cyrillic and Latin script, and is available in Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro and North Macedonia. Parts of these terms marked coming describe what the game has yet to get.

By using the game you accept these terms and the privacy policy. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the game.

2. Age

Only people aged 16 and over may use the game. We may delete an account when we learn that it belongs to someone younger.

3. Your account

  • You play at once, as a guest. A guest is tied to the device: if, before registering, you remove the app or its data, or do not play for 30 days, the guest is gone, points and all. A registered account is not lost that way: after 30 days without playing, you only log in again.
  • Registering is optional: you choose a username and a password and keep everything you have as a guest.
  • If you log in to an account you already have on a device where you play as a guest, the guest's points do not move to the account. The game warns you before you log in.
  • A username must not insult anyone or pass itself off as someone else. Keep your password to yourself: you are responsible for what is done through your account.
  • We do not ask for an email address, so we cannot recover a forgotten password: in that case, make a new account.
  • coming In the Android version you will also be able to sign in with Google Play Games, Google's gaming service. An account linked that way is registered, just as one with a username is. The link between your Google Play Games profile and your account in the game is permanent: for now it cannot be undone, except by deleting the account. If the profile is already linked to another account in the game, signing in switches you to that account, and the guest's points stay behind. Sign in only with your own profile: whoever signs in with your profile plays as you.

4. Points

  • You earn a point for every answer and for every like on your questions, and lose it when a like is taken back. A dislike neither earns nor costs points.
  • Sending in a question costs points, as many as the send button shows, which you get back if the question is rejected.
  • Points have no money value. They cannot be bought, sold, exchanged for money or goods, or transferred to another player.
  • We may change how points are scored and what a question costs. We may cancel points earned by breaking these terms.

5. Question rules

Registered players can send in questions: those with a username or, once it arrives, those signed in with Google Play Games. Sending one costs points, and you can have at most 20 questions waiting for review at once.

A question must:

  • be a real dilemma: two different options, each one something a person might really pick;
  • be short: each answer on one line, 200 characters at most;
  • be written in Serbian so that each answer answers the question „Шта би радије?“ ("What would you rather?"): with an infinitive („Летети кад год пожелиш“, "Fly whenever you like") or a clause with „да“ („Да ти храна увек буде мало пресољена“, "Have your food always a little too salty"), with no word that depends on whether the player is a man or a woman (not „Бити богат“, but „Имати много пара“, "Have lots of money").

Topics we do not take for now

A question must not be about religion, politics, health or sexuality. The moderator rejects such questions however they are written, even when they insult nobody. Should we allow them later, it will say so here.

A question must not contain:

  • hate speech, insults or degradation over nationality, religion, sex, sexual orientation, skin colour, disability or any other personal characteristic;
  • real private persons: friends, neighbours, teachers, colleagues or anyone who can be recognized. Well-known public figures are fine, but without insults, false claims or degradation;
  • personal data: names, phone numbers, addresses, usernames or links;
  • spam, ads or promotion, or the same question sent in more than once;
  • violence, threats, or encouragement of self-harm or of dangerous or illegal acts;
  • sexual content of any kind, and never, in any form, content that sexualizes minors;
  • someone else's text that you have no right to publish.

Rights in your question

Your question stays yours. By sending it in, you give us a free, non-exclusive right, without limit in time or territory, to store it, show it in the game, file it under categories, translate it into the game's languages, and keep it in the game after your account is deleted, without your name.

6. Moderation

  • A moderator reviews every question before it goes into the game, and approves or rejects it. A rejection comes with a short reason. You see where your questions stand in the app, under My questions, and, coming, in a notification on your phone.
  • The moderator sees your questions without your username, but knows which questions share an author.
  • The moderator can change a question's categories but not its text. So a question that breaks the rules is rejected, and you can send in a corrected one.
  • We may later take an approved question out of the game, for example after reports. What it cost to send is not refunded then, and the points it has already earned stay.
  • We may stop someone who sends in questions that break the rules from sending more. Their questions waiting for review are then rejected, and what sending them cost is refunded.
  • We decide about questions at our own discretion. If you think we got it wrong, write to us.

7. Reporting and hiding coming

Each question on the game screen will have a menu where you can:

  • report the question to the moderator, with a reason from a list: it is offensive, it is about a real person, it is spam, it is not a real dilemma, or something else. A report also hides the question from you;
  • hide the question: you are not shown it again;
  • hide its author: you are not shown any of their questions again, including those approved later.

Each of these applies to you alone, and for good: for now nothing hidden can be brought back. The author stays anonymous: you do not learn who they are, and they do not learn that you reported or hid them. Starter questions have no author, so hiding the author of one hides only that question. The moderator reviews reported questions and decides whether to take a question out of the game. Reporting and hiding neither earn nor cost points. Please do not misuse reports.

Until reporting reaches the game, or if you cannot report a question in the game, write to us.

8. Starter questions and sample votes

The game starts with questions we wrote ourselves. So that the first answers do not show 100%, every starter question was given a made-up starting number of votes for both sides, different for each question. The percentages on those questions count these starting votes as well as players' votes. Questions sent in by players have no starting votes.

9. Fair play

  • exploit bugs in the game rather than report them to us;
  • attack the service, get around its limits, or reach it other than through the game;
  • pass yourself off as someone else.

If someone breaks these terms, we may cancel their points, remove their questions, stop them sending in questions, and limit or delete their account.
