Account deletion

You can delete your account in Would You Rather? (Шта би радије?; developer: [ИМЕ И ПРЕЗИМЕ / NAME]) by email, and soon in the app. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

By email, without the app

Email [EMAIL] with the subject "Account deletion" and the account's username. We will delete it within 30 days at the latest and let you know when we have. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours, for example by naming one of your questions. We will never ask for your password.

A guest has no username, so we cannot find a guest account by email: delete it in the app. If the app is already gone, or has not been used for 30 days, no device leads to the guest any more, and it stays on the server for [РОК / PERIOD].

In the app coming

Deleting in the app is being built. Once it arrives, it will go like this:

  1. Open the game and tap the account icon at the top right.
  2. On the Account screen (Налог), tap Delete account (Обриши налог).
  3. Confirm.

The account is deleted at once, on every device, and on the device you delete it from the game carries on as a new guest. This deletes a guest and an account alike, whether it has a username or is linked to Google Play Games.

A guest you log in to another account from, on some device, stays a player of its own and is not deleted with that account. If you do not want it to stay, delete it in the app before you log in.

What is deleted

  • the player and its id, the username and the password hash, points and stats;
  • every session, so you are logged out on every device;
  • answers, with their times, skipped questions and reactions, so the counts on questions no longer include them; every like you gave is taken back, along with the point it earned the question's author;
  • questions waiting for review and rejected questions, with their reasons;
  • coming reports of questions, hidden questions and authors, notification tokens, and the link to Google Play Games.

What stays

  • Approved questions, including those taken out of the game, stay in the game without an author: nothing in the database links them to you any more. Other players' answers and reactions to them stay, and players who hid you as an author still do not see them, without learning who you are.
  • Analytics: events already sent to PostHog stay tied to the device's random id and, if you registered or logged in, to your player id, which after the deletion names nobody in our database. They are deleted after [РОК / PERIOD].
  • Server logs, which may hold your player id, and database backups, if any, until they expire, [РОК / PERIOD].
  • Data the law requires us to keep, only for as long as it requires.

Removing the app from a device does not by itself delete the account.
