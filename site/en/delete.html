Account deletion

You can delete your account in Would You Rather? (Шта би радије?; developer: [ИМЕ И ПРЕЗИМЕ / NAME]) in the app or by email. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Open the game and tap the account icon at the top right.
  2. On the Account screen (Налог), tap Delete account (Обриши налог).
  3. Confirm.

The account is deleted at once, and on that device the game carries on as a new guest. This works for a guest and for an account with a username alike.

By email, without the app

Email [EMAIL] with the subject "Account deletion" and the account's username. We will delete it within 30 days at the latest and let you know when we have. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours, for example by naming one of your questions. We will never ask for your password.

A guest has no username, so we cannot find a guest account by email: delete it in the app. If the app is already gone, the guest is deleted automatically after 90 days unused.

What is deleted

  • the player and its id, the username and the password hash;
  • every session, so you are logged out on every device;
  • points and stats;
  • answers, answer times, skipped questions and reactions, so the counts on questions no longer include them;
  • questions waiting for review and rejected questions, with their reasons;
  • your reports of questions;
  • the notification token, the link to Google Play Games and the personalization data.

What stays

  • Approved questions, including those taken out of the game, stay in the game without an author: nothing in the database links them to you any more. Other players' answers and reactions to them stay.
  • Analytics: events are tied to a random id, not to the account, and are deleted after [РОК / PERIOD].
  • Server logs and database backups, if any, until they expire, [РОК / PERIOD].
  • Data the law requires us to keep, only for as long as it requires.

Removing the app from a device does not by itself delete the account.

More about your data is in the privacy policy.
